Long Run privacy policy

Updated 27 October 2026. This policy covers Lothringen: The Long Run, listed on Google Play as Long Run, and its separate Long Run Staging and Long Run Dev apps. Each app has its own account and progression. It also covers this game's public information pages and browser-based local Practice game.

Who is responsible

Nicolas Louis operates the game and is responsible for the personal data processed to provide it. For privacy, support or account deletion, email lothringen.rpg@gmail.com.

What we process and why

Your account and game progress

The Android game uses Google Play Games sign-in and Firebase Authentication. We receive the game-related identity needed to link your Play Games profile to a game account. We process account identifiers, authentication tokens, your chosen profile name, equipment, abilities, inventory, currency, inbox and progression to sign you in, save your progress and provide the game features you request. We do not receive your Google password.

Online runs include an account-linked run identifier, level and seed, tick-stamped game inputs, replay files, scores, validation results and reward records. The server replays these inputs to check results and award rewards consistently. Leaderboard names, scores and selected ghost replays can be shown to other players. Ghost replays contain gameplay, not video or audio recordings.

These account and gameplay operations are necessary to provide the game service you request. Without the required sign-in and run data, the Android online game cannot provide those features.

Security and reliability

We process app/device attestation results, request and network metadata, quota counters, validation outcomes and restricted operational logs to prevent abuse, protect accounts, investigate failures and operate the service. Our legitimate interests are a secure, fair and reliable game.

Staging and production Android releases use Firebase Crashlytics for crash and error diagnosis. Reports can include stack traces, app and operating system versions, device characteristics and installation/session identifiers. Our Dart error reporting removes exception messages and does not add your game account ID or game request payloads. Native SDK reports have their own documented fields. Dev builds do not enable our Crashlytics reporting.

We do not use advertising SDKs, Google Analytics or cross-app advertising identifiers in this beta. We do not sell your personal data or use your Play Games data for advertising.

Local data and support

Controls, local Practice selections and pending-run recovery state are stored on your device. The browser Practice game does not sign in or submit online runs. Loading the website still sends normal web requests to its hosting provider.

If you contact us, we process your email address and the information you send to answer your request. Service support is part of providing the game; handling privacy rights requests also fulfils our legal obligations. Please do not send passwords, sign-in codes or authentication tokens.

Who receives data and where it is processed

Google provides Play Games, Play Integrity, Firebase Authentication, App Check, Firestore, Cloud Storage, Cloud Functions, Cloud Run, Crashlytics and Hosting. We use these services for the purposes above. Support correspondence is handled through Gmail. Access to administrative game data is limited to people who need it to operate or support the game. We may disclose information when legally required.

The staging and production game databases and replay services are located in Google's Europe West region. This does not mean all processing stays in Europe: Firebase Authentication is operated in the United States, and other Google services can process data internationally. Google's applicable processing terms describe transfer safeguards, including standard contractual clauses where applicable. See Firebase privacy and security, Firebase data processing terms and Google's privacy policy. You can contact us for information about the safeguards applicable to your data.

How long data is kept

Your account and progression are kept while your game account exists. Run, reward and competitive records support that account and the relevant game features; cleanup may remove individual records earlier. Deleting an account removes its associated active game data through the deletion process below. Separate retention periods apply to restricted recovery, security and provider records:

  • A minimal account-keyed deletion record expires 30 days after completion and is removed by scheduled cleanup. A backlog or outage can delay removal.
  • Game database recovery copies and deleted replay/ghost objects can remain for up to seven days under the configured recovery policies.
  • Operational service logs are kept for 30 days; required Google Cloud administrative and system audit logs are kept for 400 days.
  • Firebase retains Crashlytics reports and associated identifiers for 90 days before beginning removal from live and backup systems.
  • Firebase Authentication retains logged IP addresses for a few weeks. After an authentication account is deleted, other authentication data is removed from its live and backup systems within 180 days.
  • Firebase Hosting retains IP-address data for a few months for security and service operation.

Support correspondence is retained while needed to resolve your request. Contact us if you want it deleted. Where a legal obligation requires specific information to be retained, we restrict its use to that purpose.

Your choices and rights

You can request access to or a copy of your personal data, correction, deletion, restriction of processing and, where applicable, data portability. You can object to processing based on legitimate interests. These rights depend on the circumstances and applicable law.

Email lothringen.rpg@gmail.com and specify the app and your in-game profile. We may ask for proportionate information to verify account ownership, without asking for your password. We normally respond within one month; if a lawful extension is needed, we will explain it within that period. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

To delete your game account, open your profile, choose Delete account, and complete both confirmations, or use the external account-deletion request route. Deletion runs asynchronously and can take several hours. It removes the selected app's account and associated progression, items, wallet, inbox, run/reward records, leaderboard entries, replays and ghost data. It does not delete your Google account, Play Games profile or accounts in the other Long Run apps. Uninstalling alone does not delete cloud data.

Automated replay checks determine game results and rewards. If you believe a result is wrong, contact support so it can be investigated. We do not use these checks to make decisions with legal or similarly significant effects.

Age audience and changes

This beta is intended for players aged 16 and over. If you believe a child under 16 has provided personal data through the game, contact us so we can investigate and remove it where appropriate.

We will update this page when our practices change and identify the update date above. Material changes will also be communicated through an appropriate game or release notice before they take effect.
