Delete your Long Run account

You can request deletion of your account and associated data for Lothringen: The Long Run, listed as Long Run on Google Play. You do not need to reinstall the app to make a request.

Request deletion by email

Email lothringen.rpg@gmail.com with the subject “Long Run account deletion”. Include:

  • Your in-game profile name and the Play Games profile you used.
  • Which app you used: Long Run, Long Run Staging, or Long Run Dev. Each has a separate account and progress.
  • A clear request to delete that account and its associated game data.

We may ask you to verify that you control the account before processing the request. Never send your password, sign-in codes, or authentication tokens. If you cannot identify the account, explain that in your email so we can help you locate it.

Delete from the app

Open your profile, choose Delete account, and complete both confirmation steps. Once the request is accepted, access is blocked while deletion runs. This can take several hours. You cannot create a new game account with the same Play Games profile until deletion finishes.

What is removed

Deletion removes the selected app's sign-in account, profile, cloud progression, owned items and wallet, inbox data, run and reward records, leaderboard entries, replays and associated ghost data. It is permanent and does not delete your Google account or your Play Games profile.

Uninstalling the app alone does not delete its cloud account. Deleting one environment's account does not delete accounts in the other apps.

What can remain temporarily

  • A minimal account-keyed deletion record prevents late work from restoring erased data. It contains the deletion state and request, completion and expiry times. It expires 30 days after completion and is removed by scheduled cleanup; a backlog or service outage can delay physical removal.
  • Restricted database recovery copies and deleted replay/ghost objects can remain for up to seven days under the configured recovery policies. They are not available for ordinary gameplay.
  • Crash reports are processed separately by Firebase Crashlytics. Firebase retains them for 90 days before beginning removal from live and backup systems. Reports are not tagged with your game account ID. See Firebase's privacy and retention information.
  • Operational service logs are retained for 30 days. Google Cloud's required administrative and system audit logs are retained for 400 days. These restricted logs support security and service operation and are separate from your playable account.

For questions about a pending deletion or other data rights, contact lothringen.rpg@gmail.com.
