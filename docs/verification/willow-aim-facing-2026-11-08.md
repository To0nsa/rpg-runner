# Willow firing-facing correction — November 8, 2026

Source: local changes based on `263ef5e9`, prepared gameplay compatibility
`2026.11.8.3`. This is local verification, not deployment or phone acceptance.

## Correction

Auto aim already used the weapon tip, but the one-tick facing lock expired
before movement checked it. Incoming movement commands also overwrote facing
on subsequent windup ticks. A running-away reproduction displaced the pistol
origin by 43.2 world units and its trajectory by 20.56 units from target center.

Weapon-tip shots now reserve facing through the scaled release tick. Both input
commands and movement honor that reservation while velocity remains controlled
normally. Cancelled or consumed intents relinquish facing; recovery does not
retain it. Manual vertical shots also reserve their selected side. Bombs and
radial-origin casters keep their existing behavior. Phase order and replay
encoding are unchanged; client, issuer and validator versions move together.
Ranked route seeds were regenerated by the existing offline producer.

## Local checks

The regression was observed failing before the fix. Core tests cover all eight
guns, both movement directions, six near/far target positions, and 30/60/120 Hz.
They compare the forecast sightline origin with every emitted pellet and verify
the center shot's trajectory. Additional cases cover manual/vertical aim, action
speed scaling, damage and mobility cancellation, and unrestricted bomb facing.
The full GameCore replay test separately caught the incoming-command overwrite;
it now checks rear firing while moving forward for every gun and compares
encoded replay results, including facing.
