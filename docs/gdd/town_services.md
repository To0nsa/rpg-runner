# Equipment and Training

Théodonis has two purchase services, both using earned account gold:

| Service | Catalogue | Categories |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Equipment store | All compatible items and ranged sources | Weapons, shields/bomb kits, spellbooks/field kits, ranged spells/weapons, accessories |
| Training Hall | Permanent character techniques | Primary, secondary, ranged techniques, mobility, jump, spells/fieldcraft |

Both serve unlocked characters. The shared gold-edged character selector initially
follows the active available character. Willow remains disabled with a lock icon
and Campaign unlock explanation until her chapter reward is earned. Her catalogue
cannot be browsed or bought before then. Browsing and purchases never change the
next run's character or saved build. Both services use the builder's category rail,
selected-entry details on the left and a compact icon/name grid on the right.
Equipment's five categories include ranged sources; Training has six technique
categories plus All categories. One centered purchase button shows the selected
offer's price, labelled Buy for Equipment or Learn for Training.
Equipment shows only items still available to buy. Owned entries are hidden;
buying removes the item and selects another available entry, or clears the
details if the category is empty. The purchase action stays centered in the
details section.
Training always includes learned techniques. Learned techniques show a
check mark on their tile and Learned in place of the purchase button; selecting
them keeps their full details inspectable. The two services are reached from
their Town entrances, without a service switch or intro.

Once a character is unlocked, all their catalogue entries are permanently
available. Each unowned item or technique costs **150 gold**. Purchases unlock
ownership; they do not automatically equip
anything. Accessories are usable by both characters and require only one account
purchase. Ranged sources and abilities retain character-specific ownership.
There are no paid refreshes, limited daily stock or new currencies.

Select an entry to inspect its details immediately, without expanding a card.
Equipment shows its own bonuses and effects. Equipment does not show
saved-build comparisons or stat deltas. Long effects use full-width descriptions,
and stat rows stack when enlarged text needs them. The builder's Town
shortcut opens Equipment directly at the edited character, category and build,
without changing the next-run selection. On narrow or enlarged-text layouts,
categories, catalogue and details form one scrolling page. On landscape screens,
details and the catalogue scroll independently while the purchase button stays visible.
Ability details explain behavior, resource costs, cooldown and requirements from
the existing gameplay definitions. Willow's resource is labelled Supplies.
The gold balance includes locally journaled rewards; buying with unconfirmed
funds opens the existing reward-verification dialog and requires a later explicit
purchase. Back returns to Town when entering from the map, or to the builder
when following its Equipment shortcut.

The catalogue supports choosing a build and saving toward its components. Gold
remains the acquisition constraint. The 150-gold baseline does not establish
economy balance; review unlocks per run and time to a chosen build during
playtesting. Rotating discounts are a possible later feature and are not active.

Library discoveries and Hall of Honour achievements retain their existing rules.
Training Hall is a purchase service, distinct from the Training Field used to
practice a saved build. Service placements are navigation choices, not new canon.
